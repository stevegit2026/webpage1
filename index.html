Five Storylines

Ranked by impact for Dec. 6

  1. Opponent

    The Cardinals are 3-8, fired a coordinator and may start a 40-year-old at QB

    Arizona is 3–8 and has lost three straight, including 34–7 at Kansas City on Nov. 22. Coach Mike LaFleur fired defensive coordinator Nick Rallis on Monday and named Teryl Austin interim coordinator, and said “we’re not going to the playoffs.” On Friday he ruled rookie quarterback Carson Beck (back spasms) out for Sunday’s home game against Washington, so 40-year-old Josh Johnson makes a second straight start, per the AP. LaFleur said Beck could return against Philadelphia and that practice next week will decide. Running back Jeremiyah Love, the No. 3 pick, is eligible to leave injured reserve with an ankle sprain; LaFleur had no update Friday and expects to know more Wednesday, per FOX Sports/Rotowire. The Cardinals are winless in four home games, per Predictem, and the Eagles may face either quarterback. Tight end Trey McBride, with 87 catches, is the one dependable weapon. The Eagles won the 2022 meeting 20–17 in Arizona, and the Cardinals won in Philadelphia 35–31 in 2023, per HawkBlogger.

  2. Last game · Comeback

    A 39–38 win in Dallas is the biggest Thanksgiving comeback ever

    The Eagles trailed 31–10 with 3:35 left in the third quarter and won 39–38 on Jake Elliott’s 59-yard field goal with two seconds left, the largest comeback in Thanksgiving history, per the Inquirer and the AP. ESPN Analytics gave Dallas a 99.4% chance to win at the 21-point mark. Joe Milton’s 50- and 45-yard touchdown passes to DeVonta Smith started it, Kenny Moore scored on a punt Ty Robinson blocked, and Milton added a rushing touchdown and a 24-yard touchdown pass to Makai Lemon. A failed two-point try left it 38–36. Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer then went for it on fourth-and-1 from the Philadelphia 39 with 55 seconds left, and Robinson sacked Dak Prescott. The Eagles are 4–7 with two straight wins after a seven-game skid. Sources differ on small details: the AP puts Milton’s rushing score at 14 yards, Sky Sports at 15, and the Inquirer’s photo caption calls the kick 61 yards while its text says 59.

  3. Quarterback

    Milton goes 21 of 31 for 331 yards against his old team

    Joe Milton III, signed off Dallas’s practice squad on Oct. 16, went 21 of 31 for 331 yards, three touchdowns and one interception in his third career start, per the Inquirer, and he ran for a score. Smith caught 11 passes for 200 yards, per the Yahoo Sports/SB Nation recap. Milton is 2–1 as a starter after going 1–1 with 30 total points in his first two outings, per DallasCowboys.com. Before Thursday the Eagles were averaging 17 points a game, per SB Nation, and the 39 they scored included 22 in the fourth quarter. Rotowire has him starting against Arizona, and PhillyVoice’s Jimmy Kempski writes the Eagles will want him on the 2027 roster, even if only as the backup. Jalen Hurts remains on injured reserve with blood clots in his lungs; none of the articles read this week gives a new update on him. Tanner McKee, who lost three starts earlier this season, remains the backup.

  4. Eagles injuries · Roster

    Goedert’s arm is in a sling and Smith left limping

    Three Eagles starters were ruled out during Thursday’s game. Cornerback Cooper DeJean was concussed four plays in, per the Inquirer. Tight end Dallas Goedert hurt his right shoulder and was ruled out in the second quarter; he left with his right arm in a sling, per The Athletic’s Zach Berman. PhillyVoice calls that a clear sign he will probably miss time, and NBC Sports notes Nick Sirianni did not comment, so a diagnosis may wait until next week. Safety Marcus Epps (foot) was carted off. Running back Saquon Barkley, held to 7 yards on 5 carries, left with a stinger the team listed as a neck injury; PhillyVoice counts four games this season in which injuries limited him to single-digit carries. DeVonta Smith was seen limping badly after landing on his tailbone, per the Delaware News Journal via CBS Sports. Gabe Hall hurt his wrist, and Jonathan Jones and Arnold Ebiketie were out before kickoff. No outlet reports an official Dec. 6 status. Zach Ertz played 29 tight end snaps Thursday and rookie Eli Stowers 21.
