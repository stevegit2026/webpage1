Five Storylines

Ranked by impact for Dec. 6

  1. Opponent

    The Cardinals are 3-8 and just fired their defensive coordinator

    Arizona is 3–8 and has lost three straight, including 34–7 at Kansas City on Nov. 22. Hogs Haven counts an 85–16 scoring deficit over those three games and about 175 yards of offense a game. Coach Mike LaFleur fired defensive coordinator Nick Rallis on Monday and named Teryl Austin interim coordinator, per Yahoo Sports. The Cardinals are winless in four home games, per Predictem, and they host Washington on Sunday, so this record can change before kickoff here. Rookie running back Jeremiyah Love, the No. 3 pick, is on injured reserve with an ankle sprain; LaFleur said the team would reassess him Monday for a possible return against Philadelphia, or wait until after the Week 14 bye, per Heavy via Yahoo. Tight end Trey McBride, with 87 catches, is the one dependable weapon. The Eagles won the 2022 meeting 20–17 in Arizona, and the Cardinals won the 2023 game in Philadelphia 35–31, per HawkBlogger.

  2. Last game · Comeback

    A 39–38 win in Dallas is the biggest Thanksgiving comeback ever

    The Eagles trailed 31–10 with 3:35 left in the third quarter and won 39–38 on Jake Elliott’s 59-yard field goal with two seconds left, the largest comeback in Thanksgiving history, per the Inquirer and the AP. ESPN Analytics gave Dallas a 99.4% chance to win at the 21-point mark. Joe Milton’s 50- and 45-yard touchdown passes to DeVonta Smith started it, Kenny Moore scored on a punt Ty Robinson blocked, and Milton added a rushing touchdown and a 24-yard touchdown pass to Makai Lemon. A failed two-point try left it 38–36. Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer then went for it on fourth-and-1 from the Philadelphia 39 with 55 seconds left, and Robinson sacked Dak Prescott. The Eagles are 4–7 with two straight wins after a seven-game skid. Sources differ on small details: the AP puts Milton’s rushing score at 14 yards, Sky Sports at 15, and the Inquirer’s photo caption calls the kick 61 yards while its text says 59.

  3. Quarterback

    Milton goes 21 of 31 for 331 yards against his old team

    Joe Milton III, signed off Dallas’s practice squad on Oct. 16, went 21 of 31 for 331 yards, three touchdowns and one interception in his third career start, per the Inquirer, and he ran for a score. Smith caught 11 passes for 200 yards, per the Yahoo Sports/SB Nation recap. Milton is 2–1 as a starter after going 1–1 with 30 total points in his first two outings, per DallasCowboys.com. Before Thursday the Eagles were averaging 17 points a game, per SB Nation, and the 39 they scored included 22 in the fourth quarter. The SB Nation writer says Milton probably earned himself a payday from some NFL team, whether or not it is the Eagles. Jalen Hurts remains on injured reserve with blood clots in his lungs; none of the articles read this week gives a new update on him. Tanner McKee, who lost three starts earlier this season, remains the backup.

  4. Eagles injuries · Roster

    DeJean, Goedert, Epps and Barkley all left the game hurt

    Three Eagles starters were ruled out during Thursday’s game. Cornerback Cooper DeJean was concussed four plays in and ruled out, per the Inquirer. Tight end Dallas Goedert hurt his shoulder and was downgraded to out in the second quarter. Safety Marcus Epps left with a foot injury in the third quarter, and Kenny Moore moved to safety, per the Inquirer. Running back Saquon Barkley, who had just 7 yards on 5 carries, left with a stinger and did not return, per USA Today; NBC Sports Philadelphia noted he had one last week and earlier this season. Defensive tackle Gabe Hall (wrist) was also listed as questionable to return. Jonathan Jones and Arnold Ebiketie had already been ruled out before the game. No outlet read by Friday morning reports an official status for any of them for Dec. 6, and the Eagles’ first injury report of the week comes later. The extra days between games should help.

  5. The line · Odds

    Philadelphia is a field-goal favorite in Arizona, a 12-point swing from Thursday

    The Eagles are about 3.5-point road favorites, according to a median of four books on sportsanalysis.info (updated 6:02 AM ET Friday); the line opened at Eagles −2.5 on Nov. 24, and the total moved from 36.5 to 37.5. DraftKings has Philadelphia at −3, BetRivers and Caesars at −3.5. Moneylines are −180 and −170 for the Eagles, which implies about a 60% chance once the vig is removed. HawkBlogger shows the Eagles by 3 with a 36.5 total but rates the game Cardinals by 2.1 in its own model, and sportsanalysis.info’s model leans Arizona by 4. One page, LineTerminal, still lists Eagles −8.5 and a 43.5 total, but it shows both teams with old records, so this page treats it as stale. Lines are early: Arizona plays Washington on Sunday, and rookie quarterback Carson Beck’s back could change the number. The Eagles are 3–8 against the spread and the Cardinals 4–7, per sportsanalysis.info.
