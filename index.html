Five Storylines

Ranked by impact on Nov. 22

  1. Last game · Milton debut

    Milton’s first start ends 19–11, and the losing streak hits seven

    The Eagles (2-7) lost 19-11 to the Giants on Sunday, their seventh straight defeat and the longest since an eight-game skid in 2012, per the AP. Joe Milton went 18 of 34 for 169 yards and led the team with 64 rushing yards, and his two-point run after Saquon Barkley’s 3-yard touchdown made it 8-7. But the Eagles scored only a field goal in the second half and have not scored a touchdown after halftime since Week 6, per PhiladelphiaEagles.com. Barkley averaged 1.7 yards per carry, and Milton was sacked seven times, per the Inquirer, which graded the offense “broken beyond repair” and the coaching D-minus. The defense had six sacks, including Jonathan Greenard’s first as an Eagle. Nick Sirianni did not rule out staff changes during the bye, telling reporters “we’ll talk” and that “it’s not Sean, it’s all of us,” per ESPN; on Monday he said none had been made yet, per the Inquirer. The Eagles’ recap listed the Giants at 5-3-1 that day, when they were 5-2-1; Thursday’s 24-21 loss to Washington made the recap’s number true.

  2. Trade deadline · Passed 4 PM ET

    Davis is the only Eagle sold, and the Ebiketie deal collapses

    The deadline passed at 4 p.m. ET with the Eagles (2-7) having made one big sale: defensive tackle Jordan Davis went to Jacksonville on Monday for a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick. A second deal, edge rusher Arnold Ebiketie to Cincinnati for a 2027 sixth-round pick, was agreed Tuesday morning, but the Bengals called it off because it would have cost them a third-round compensatory pick, per NFL.com and NBC Sports Philadelphia. Ebiketie stays. So do cornerback Riq Woolen, tight end Dallas Goedert, linebacker Zack Baun, guard Landon Dickerson and receiver Hollywood Brown, per The Eagles Wire. Several teams asked about Woolen, but no offer made sense, and The Athletic’s Zach Berman reports Goedert’s contract structure made an in-season trade costly. Inside The Iggles calls the day “anticlimactic,” says Woolen and Brown could have fetched Day 3 picks, and lists the earlier Cam Jurgens trade to Baltimore as the other in-season sale. Whether the Eagles can extend Moro Ojomo remains open.

  3. Eagles injuries

    Smith and Wicks keep missing games, Baun hurts his back, and Lane Johnson is gone

    The Eagles go into the Week 10 bye with a long list. Receivers DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) missed a third straight game Sunday, per the Inquirer and NBC Sports. Linebacker Zack Baun, perhaps the team’s best defender with two sacks, left the game with a back injury, per the Inquirer, and there is no status update yet. Barkley played through hamstring and back injuries and had been limited to 32 offensive snaps over the previous two weeks, per NBC Sports. Linebacker Jihaad Campbell (hamstring and quad, two games missed, per the Inquirer), cornerbacks Jonathan Jones and Michael Carter II and receiver Britain Covey were ruled out Friday. Right tackle Lane Johnson retired Oct. 6, citing his mental health, per Yahoo Sports; an earlier version of this page labeled his absence “personal,” which was incomplete. Jalen Hurts is on injured reserve with blood clots in his lungs, and the AP calls him likely out for the season. Smith has played just four games, last on Oct. 18, and Wicks six, per the Inquirer’s Nov. 12 outlook. The next official report comes the week of Nov. 16, so every status here is a pre-bye snapshot.

  4. Opponent · Steelers

    The 6-2 Steelers come off a bye leading the AFC North on the strength of the run game

    Pittsburgh is 6-2 and first in the AFC North after a 27-6 win over Cleveland in Week 8, then a Week 9 bye. Jaylen Warren has 998 scrimmage yards, the most by a Steelers player through eight games since 2018, per NFL.com, and the defense has allowed 55 rushing yards per game during a four-game winning streak. The receiver room is the question: Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson are on injured reserve, and DK Metcalf is on pace for a career-low receiving total, per USA Today. Lineman Derrick Harmon, limited Wednesday and Thursday, practiced fully Friday and is questionable; every other Steeler practiced fully with no game status, per Steelers Depot. GM Omar Khan stood pat at the deadline; Brooke Pryor, via Sportsnet Pittsburgh, says the Steelers will not spend significant draft capital without a plan at quarterback after Rodgers. Before Philadelphia, they visit the 6-2 Bengals on Sunday night, Nov. 15, as 3.5-point underdogs, per VSiN via Steelers Depot. They have not won in Philadelphia since 1965, per SI. Sources disagree on the Week 3 score against Cincinnati: Steelers Depot says 30-27 Pittsburgh, while SI says 27-24.

    Books now make the Steelers 4.5-point favorites, and the Eagles sit near plus-165

Expect movement after the Steelers play at Cincinnati on Nov. 15 and the first injury reports arrive.
