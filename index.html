Five Storylines

Ranked by impact on Nov. 22

  1. Opponent · Last game

    The Steelers arrive 6-3 after a 38–18 beating in Cincinnati

    Pittsburgh lost 38-18 at Cincinnati on Sunday night, per the Bengals and NBC Sports, dropping to 6-3 while the 7-2 Bengals took sole possession of first place in the AFC North. Aaron Rodgers started 0 of 8 and finished 15 of 29 for 143 yards and two touchdowns, both in the fourth quarter after the game was decided; he was sacked three times. Joe Burrow threw for 409 yards and four touchdowns, and Ja’Marr Chase caught 11 passes for 231 yards. The Steelers had four first downs and 40 yards at halftime, per NBC Sports. Steelers Depot says it was the most points Pittsburgh has allowed since 2024. Mike McCarthy called the result “not to the standard of the Pittsburgh Steelers,” per SB Nation via Yahoo. Safety DeShon Elliott was a late scratch after a knee problem showed up in warmups; McCarthy gave no detail, per ClutchPoints. Sources differ on the halftime score: Bengals.com and NBC say 24-3, SB Nation says 24-7. The Steelers have not won in Philadelphia since 1965, per SI.

  2. Eagles coaching · Bye-week decisions

    No staff changes yet, and the Eagles’ offense is still near the bottom

    Nick Sirianni did not rule out coaching changes after the 19-11 loss to the Giants, saying “we’ll talk,” and a day later said none had been made while the staff worked through a self-scout, per the Inquirer. No source read reports a change since. First-year coordinator Sean Mannion, 34, has the offense 30th in scoring and 32nd in yards, per Pro Football Rumors, and the Inquirer says he “may not survive” the season, with line coach Chris Kuper also under scrutiny. Sirianni says the blame “starts with me.” Sources disagree on what comes next: the Inquirer’s Jeff McLane called a bye-week change “all but definite” on Nov. 9, while the Delaware News Journal argued there is little point in firing anyone with the Eagles slotted for the No. 5 pick, per Tankathon. The Eagles average 16.4 points a game and allow 24.6, per ESPN’s standings. Sirianni’s next news conference should say whether Mannion keeps the play-calling for Sunday’s game against a Pittsburgh team that gives up 20.2.

  3. Quarterback

    Milton looks set to start again, with Hurts out and McKee benched

    Joe Milton III made his first career start against the Giants: 18 of 34 for 169 yards, a team-high 64 rushing yards, and seven sacks taken, per the Inquirer and PhiladelphiaEagles.com. Steelers Now, via Yahoo, says it appears Milton will start again, but no Eagles source read has named a Week 11 starter. NFL.com notes he had been in town only a few weeks; the Delaware News Journal says the Eagles signed him off Dallas’ practice squad on Oct. 16. Tanner McKee lost all three of his starts before being benched, per SteelerNation. Jalen Hurts is on injured reserve with blood clots in his lungs and has not played since Week 5. SI says he is out for the season; the Inquirer says only “likely.” The Athletic noted Nov. 4 that McKee is in the last year of his deal, Milton is a restricted free agent and rookie Cole Payton has three years left, which could shape late-season playing time. The Eagles Wire lists Milton as “unsettled” and says the bye gave him preparation time.

    Read at PhiladelphiaEagles.com →
  4. Eagles injuries · Roster

    The Eagles wait on new injury reports, with two running backs possibly back

    The Eagles have not released an injury report since their Nov. 6 Friday list, and the first for Pittsburgh should come this week. ESPN’s game page still shows DeVonta Smith (hamstring), Dontayvion Wicks (ankle), Jonathan Jones (hamstring), Britain Covey (knee) and Michael Carter II (abdomen) as questionable, but that appears to be the old report carried over, not new information. Smith has played four games all year and missed the last three, per the Inquirer. Zack Baun left the Giants game with a back injury and has no update. Running backs Tank Bigsby and Will Shipley have each missed the required four games on injured reserve, and SI reported they could return Nov. 22, though no move is official. Saquon Barkley has 296 yards on 69 carries, per NBC Sports Philadelphia. The roster that does play includes rookie Makai Lemon, who leads the team with 37 catches for 312 yards, and Darius Cooper, with four touchdown catches, per the Eagles Wire. Hollywood Brown and Fred Johnson were waived Friday, per Yahoo Sports.

  5. The line · Odds

    Books still make the Steelers 4.5-point favorites after the Cincinnati blowout

    Sportsbooks have not moved off Pittsburgh minus-4.5 since Nov. 10, and ESPN’s game page this morning, after the Steelers’ loss, still shows the Steelers at minus-4.5 with a 38.5 total and moneylines of minus-198 and plus-164. That ESPN page also shows an opener of Eagles minus-5.5 with a 42.5 total, which explains the old Eagles-favored numbers floating around. Scores and Odds lists the Steelers at minus-4.5 across its books, with 72% of bets on Pittsburgh. Removing the vig, the moneylines imply about 63.7% for the Steelers; ESPN Analytics says 63.6% for Pittsburgh and 36.1% for Philadelphia. Polymarket showed the Steelers at 69% on a page cached around 10 PM ET Sunday, which may be before the final whistle in Cincinnati. Expect movement this week as bettors weigh the blowout against a 2-7 team on its third quarterback. Scores and Odds shows the Eagles 1-8 against the spread,; the Steelers were 6-2 against it before Sunday.
