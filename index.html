Five Storylines

Ranked by impact on Sunday

  1. Quarterback · Milton prep

    Sirianni explains the Milton switch and admits he held the news back

    Nick Sirianni spoke Friday for the first time since benching Tanner McKee for Joe Milton III. He said McKee “played solid,” but the move was what he felt the team needed “at this particular moment to give ourselves the best chance to win this weekend,” per NBC Sports Philadelphia. He praised Milton’s “very talented arm” and athleticism and said he can “process and make decisions.” Milton, 26, was signed off the Cowboys’ practice squad on Oct. 16 and will make his first NFL start. On the leak, Sirianni said he held the information “as long as I possibly could,” even after saying Monday, “Tanner’s our guy,” because every minute helps the opponent less, per Pro Football Talk and the Inquirer. The Eagles are 32nd in yards and 30th in scoring, per NBC Sports Philadelphia. McKee, 0-3 as the starter, is officially questionable with a rib injury, though Giants.com’s table lists chest. Jalen Hurts remains out for the season.

  2. Eagles injuries

    Smith and Wicks are out; Barkley is cleared to play

    The Eagles ruled out six players Friday: receivers DeVonta Smith (hamstring), Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) and Britain Covey (knee), linebacker Jihaad Campbell (hamstring/quad), defensive back Michael Carter (abdomen) and cornerback Jonathan Jones (hamstring), per PhiladelphiaEagles.com. Smith will miss his fifth game in six weeks and Wicks his third straight. Makai Lemon and Darius Cooper started at receiver last week against Washington. Saquon Barkley (hamstring/back) was limited Friday with no designation, and the team site says he is “good to go.” The Inquirer’s Jeff Neiburg still expects a snap restriction. Tanner McKee (rib) is the only Eagle listed questionable. Kicker Jake Elliott and edge rusher Jonathan Greenard returned to full practice, while Jalyx Hunt stayed limited without a designation. Tackle Jordan Mailata (elbow) was full both days he was listed. NBC Sports’ betting page still lists Barkley, Smith, Wicks and Carter as questionable, which does not match the team’s final report.

    Read at PhiladelphiaEagles.com →
  3. Opponent · Giants

    Skattebo is doubtful, and Nabers is a full-go

    Giants running back Cam Skattebo (hamstring) is doubtful after a limited practice Friday, his first of the week, per Giants.com. He hurt the hamstring in overtime at Houston on Oct. 25 and sat out Wednesday and Thursday. Coach John Harbaugh did not close the door: “Let’s keep it open, let’s see what happens,” per ESPN. Devin Singletary is expected to carry the load, with newcomer Greg Desrosiers Jr. and Phil Mafah behind him, and Tyrone Tracy Jr. is on injured reserve. Skattebo leads New York with 418 rushing yards, and no other Giant has 100. Wide receiver Malik Nabers, who sat out Thursday with a knee issue, practiced fully Friday, and Harbaugh said he will be “full go.” Nabers leads the team with 48 catches for 605 yards and is chasing a fifth straight 100-yard game. Receiver and returner Charlie Jones (hand) is questionable. Every other Giant on the report, including tackles Andrew Thomas and Jermaine Eluemunor, was a full participant. New York is looking for its first win at Lincoln Financial Field since 2013.

  4. The line · Odds

    The Giants are 3 to 3.5-point favorites, and books split on the total

    The Giants are road favorites of 3 to 3.5 points, and the Milton news has barely moved the number. The Inquirer’s Thursday table has Giants minus-3 (minus-120) at FanDuel and minus-3.5 (minus-102) at DraftKings, and PhillyVoice’s Tuesday table shows BetMGM, BetRivers and Caesars at minus-3.5. USA Today’s Giants Wire says BetMGM’s spread was unchanged Friday from the minus-3 it showed to start the week, which conflicts with PhillyVoice, so I could not settle BetMGM. The total is also in conflict: Giants Wire has BetMGM at 38.5 Friday, NBC Sports and FanDuel Research show 39.5, and SI printed both numbers in separate articles. SI says the game opened Giants minus-2.5. NBC Sports puts the look-ahead line with Hurts at Eagles minus-5.5, roughly an eight-point swing. This is the first time since 2009 the Giants are favored in Philadelphia. All three Inquirer writers pick the Giants (27-20, 31-14, 27-20), and three of PhillyVoice’s four do too, with Evan Macy taking the Eagles 22-21.

  5. Last game · Trade deadline

    Six straight losses, and the Nov. 10 deadline is two days after kickoff

    The Eagles (2-6) have lost six in a row, the last two by 17 points each, and are off to their worst eight-game start since 1999, per NJ.com. Sunday’s 27-10 loss at Washington turned a 10-0 lead into 27 unanswered points. The offense averages 17.1 points per game, the fewest for any Eagles team since 2012, per the Inquirer, which has it 29th in scoring and last in yards. NBC Sports Philadelphia has it 30th in scoring and 32nd in yards, so the sources differ on rank. The Inquirer also cites a 42.6 percent pressure rate allowed, fourth-worst in the league. The trade deadline is Tuesday, Nov. 10, at 4 p.m. ET. A Bleeding Green Nation roundup lists Hollywood Brown, Riq Woolen, Dallas Goedert, Michael Carter II and now Tanner McKee as players other teams see as available, while Moro Ojomo is a different case because Howie Roseman wants him long term. 97.5 The Fanatic marked Goedert a “Stay” on Tuesday. Saquon Barkley says the team told him he will not be traded.
