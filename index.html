Five Storylines

Ranked by impact on Nov. 26

  1. Opponent

    The Cowboys come in 5-6 and red-hot on offense after scoring 51

    Dallas beat Tennessee 51-30 on Sunday, scoring on all eight possessions and touchdowns on its first six, per the AP. Dak Prescott went 28 of 33 for 325 yards and accounted for five touchdowns, and CeeDee Lamb passed 1,000 receiving yards for a sixth straight season, per the Dallas Sports Journal. The Cowboys score 30.6 points a game, third in the league per Field Level Media, but they have allowed at least 20 points in every game this season, per the Dallas Morning News. Mike Pettine called the defense for the first time Sunday. The AP says Christian Parker was “essentially demoted” after the 46-40 loss to San Francisco, but the Dallas Sports Journal says Parker remains coordinator. Dallas will be without tight end Jake Ferguson, guard Tyler Booker, safety P.J. Locke and receiver Jonathan Mingo, and cornerback DaRon Bland is on injured reserve. The Cowboys won 38-21 in Philadelphia on Oct. 26 and have won four straight Thanksgiving games, but they are 0-2 against the Eagles on the holiday, per DallasCowboys.com.

  2. Last game · Coaching

    A 19–16 win over Pittsburgh ends the skid, and the staff stays put

    The Eagles beat Pittsburgh 19-16 at Lincoln Financial Field for their first win since Sept. 20, per the AP. They led 13-0 after one quarter on two Jake Elliott field goals and a 38-yard Saquon Barkley touchdown run, then held on with Elliott’s 53- and 55-yard kicks, per the CBS Sports box score. Aaron Rodgers was sacked five times. DK Metcalf was ejected in the third quarter after a second unsportsmanlike foul. Joe Milton III’s 7-yard throw to DeVonta Smith on third-and-6 with 1:09 left sealed it. “Man, we missed this,” Nick Sirianni said. The win settled the coaching question for now. Sirianni kept Sean Mannion as play-caller and made no staff changes during the bye, per NBC Sports Philadelphia, and line coach Chris Kuper also stayed. The Eagles did bring back T.J. Paganetti as a consultant. The Inquirer’s Jeff McLane says Mannion moved Milton mostly into shotgun and pistol, cutting under-center snaps to about 12% from about 28%. Sirianni is 6-0 after byes, per the Inquirer.

  3. Quarterback

    Milton gets a third start, against the team he left in October

    Joe Milton III will make his third career start Thursday, and the Eagles signed him off the Cowboys’ practice squad on Oct. 16, per ESPN’s Tim McManus via TSN. Against Pittsburgh he went 11 of 21 for 141 yards with no touchdowns, no interceptions and a 73.7 rating, per the Inquirer, and he did not turn the ball over. Over two starts he has completed about 53% of his passes, run 14 times for 71 yards and been sacked 10 times; ESPN says the offense’s passing success rate is 27.7%. The Dallas Morning News puts him at 5.6 yards per attempt. Mannion has talked about using Milton’s legs, per PhiladelphiaEagles.com. Tanner McKee, benched after losing three starts, finished the week at full practice with a rib injury and is the backup. Jalen Hurts remains on injured reserve with blood clots in his lungs; Bleeding Green Nation says he is out for the season, and no source read from the last two weeks gives a new update on him.

    Read ESPN’s story at TSN →
  4. Eagles injuries · Roster

    Jones and Ebiketie are out; Mitchell and Barkley are cleared

    The Eagles’ final injury report rules out cornerback Jonathan Jones (ankle) and outside linebacker Arnold Ebiketie (oblique), and nobody is listed as doubtful or questionable, per PhiladelphiaEagles.com. Quinyon Mitchell, who left Sunday’s game with a hamstring injury, went from no practice to limited to full, and Vic Fangio said, “I think he’ll be fine,” per nj.com. Saquon Barkley, who left with an apparent stinger, practiced fully Tuesday and has no designation. With Jones out, SI expects Riq Woolen to start, though LehighValleyLive notes Woolen was benched earlier for missed tackles. Zack Baun and Dontayvion Wicks went on injured reserve Nov. 21 and can’t return until after Week 14, while Will Shipley came off IR and was limited all week. Tank Bigsby is eligible but still on IR. DeVonta Smith, Britain Covey and Michael Carter II practiced fully. The Eagles signed defensive tackle Gabe Hall, who had a sack Sunday, and linebacker Chance Campbell to the active roster.

  5. The line · Odds

    Dallas is an 8.5-point favorite, up from a near pick’em in May

    Every book on VegasInsider has Dallas at minus-8.5, and FanDuel’s moneylines are minus-446 and plus-348, per FOX Sports, which implies about a 78.5% chance of a Cowboys win once the vig is removed. FOX calls it the biggest line move of any Thanksgiving game. Sources disagree on where it started in May: SI and FOX say FanDuel opened Dallas minus-1.5, while Cowboys On SI and VegasInsider list the Eagles minus-1.5 at DraftKings. The total opened at 49.5 and sits at 47.5 at most books, 48 at Circa and 48.5 per Covers. The Eagles are 2-8 against the spread and 0-3 on the road, per SI Betting; the Cowboys are 5-6 against it, and their games have gone over the total eight times in 11. Analysts split: SI and ClutchPoints like Dallas minus-8.5, Covers takes the Eagles plus the points, and VSiN likes the Eagles’ team total going over 18.5. DallasCowboys.com staff all picked Dallas, by margins of 10 to 23 points.
