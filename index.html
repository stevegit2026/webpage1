Five Storylines

Ranked by impact on Sunday

  1. Quarterback · Milton prep

    Joe Milton starts, and teammates are talking up his arm

    The Eagles are benching Tanner McKee for Joe Milton III against the Giants. NBC Sports Philadelphia credits ESPN with the first report on Wednesday, 48 hours after Nick Sirianni said, “Tanner’s our guy,” per AP. McKee went 0-3 as the starter and was limited Thursday with a rib injury, after being listed Wednesday with a chest injury. Milton, 26, has played five NFL games, all in relief, completing 37 of 53 passes for 424 yards. Teammates are intrigued. Tackle Jordan Mailata called his arm “a work of art,” and receiver Darius Cooper said he now needs gloves, per NJ.com. Tight end Dallas Goedert said, “I think they’re trying to do anything to find a spark.” The Giants say they are unbothered. Slot cornerback Dru Phillips said, “It’s not a big deal,” though he noted Milton is a “willing runner.” Milton ran four times for 43 yards against New York in last season’s Week 18 for Dallas. Jalen Hurts is out for the season.

  2. Eagles injuries

    Barkley is back at practice; Smith, Wicks and four others are not

    Saquon Barkley returned to practice Thursday as a limited participant, but six Eagles still did not participate: receivers DeVonta Smith (hamstring), Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) and Britain Covey (knee), linebacker Jihaad Campbell (hamstring/quad), defensive back Michael Carter II (abdomen) and cornerback Jonathan Jones (hamstring), per Eagles Wire. Barkley missed the first two practices last week, then played 22 snaps against Washington after 10 against Dallas, per NBC Sports. Smith and Wicks have missed two straight games and are on track to miss a third. Without them, Joe Milton’s receivers would be Makai Lemon, Darius Cooper, Hollywood Brown and Elijah Moore, per NBC Sports Philadelphia. McKee was limited with a rib injury, and Mailata appeared on the report with an elbow injury but practiced fully. Kicker Jake Elliott and edge rushers Jonathan Greenard and Jalyx Hunt stayed limited. The Eagles hold their last practice Friday, then release game statuses. ESPN’s injury page already shows many Eagles as questionable, but those are placeholders, not team designations.

  3. Opponent · Giants

    Skattebo looks out, and Nabers sat with a sore knee

    Giants running back Cam Skattebo (hamstring) missed a second straight practice and appears unlikely to play Sunday, per the New York Daily News. Devin Singletary is in line to start. Wide receiver Malik Nabers, named NFC offensive player of the month after 36 catches and 509 yards in October, sat out Thursday with his surgically repaired knee but told reporters he will be “good.” Edge rusher Abdul Carter returned as a full participant after an illness. Tackles Andrew Thomas (groin) and Jermaine Eluemunor (calf) were limited again, and lineman Darius Alexander (foot) joined the report. Running back depth is thin after Tyrone Tracy Jr. went to injured reserve and Najee Harris was released, so the Giants are linked to Colts back Jonathan Taylor. NFL Network says Indianapolis wants a package like the one San Francisco paid for Christian McCaffrey, and no one has offered it yet, per NJ.com. New York also plays Thursday night at home against Washington, five days after this game.

  4. The line · Odds

    The Giants are 3 to 3.5-point favorites, and books split on the total

    The Giants are road favorites of 3 to 3.5 points, and the Milton news has barely moved the number. The Inquirer’s Thursday afternoon table has the Giants at minus-3 (minus-120) at FanDuel and minus-3.5 (minus-102) at DraftKings. Yahoo, citing BetMGM as of Thursday, also shows minus-3.5, up from minus-3 at Monday’s open. Giants moneylines run from minus-166 to minus-178. The total is in conflict: SI, FanDuel’s research page and Yahoo show 39.5, while the Inquirer lists 38.5 at both FanDuel and DraftKings. I could not settle it. The Dimers model gives the Giants a 60.8 percent win probability and a 21-18 score. The Giants are 5-2 against the spread and the Eagles 1-7, per SI. NBC Sports says the look-ahead line with Hurts was Eagles minus-5.5, an eight-to-nine-point swing. It is the first time since 2009 the Giants are favored in Philadelphia. Lines may shift on Friday’s reports.

  5. Last game · Trade deadline

    Six straight losses, and the Nov. 10 deadline is two days after kickoff

    The Eagles (2-6) have lost six in a row, the last two by 17 points each, and are off to their worst eight-game start since 1999, per NJ.com. They play the Giants before a Week 10 bye. Sunday’s 27-10 loss at Washington turned a 10-0 lead into 27 unanswered points, and the Commanders sacked McKee five times for 35 yards. Tackle Jordan Mailata said Wednesday’s focus was cleaning up communication at the line of scrimmage, with “urgency,” per the Inquirer. The trade deadline is Tuesday, Nov. 10, at 4 p.m. ET. Saquon Barkley says the team told him he will not be traded, per ESPN, and NFL Network’s Mike Garafolo reports the Eagles do not expect to move him. 97.5 The Fanatic calls Zack Baun unlikely to move and marks defensive tackle Moro Ojomo as a “Go” and tight end Dallas Goedert as a “Stay.” The Eagles already traded center Cam Jurgens to Baltimore. The Giants have lost 13 straight in Philadelphia, but John Harbaugh is 15-3 after a bye.
