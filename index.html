Five Storylines

Ranked by impact on Sunday

  1. Quarterback · Breaking Wednesday

    “Tanner’s our guy” lasted two days: Joe Milton will start

    The Eagles are benching Tanner McKee and starting Joe Milton III against the Giants, NFL Network’s Mike Garafolo and ESPN’s Adam Schefter reported Wednesday. The move came 48 hours after Nick Sirianni said, “Tanner’s our guy. We’re not making any changes there,” per AP. Players were told in a meeting, and Sirianni did not mention a change in his news conference, saying only that he likes McKee’s progression. McKee went 0-3 as the starter, completing 63.2 percent of his passes for 638 yards, two touchdowns and two interceptions. Milton, 26, signed from Dallas’ practice squad on Oct. 16 and has played in five NFL games, all in relief, completing 37 of 53 passes for 424 yards, per NFL.com. Tight end Dallas Goedert said, “I think they’re trying to do anything to find a spark.” Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy called Milton “uber talented.” Jalen Hurts is out for the season.

  2. Eagles injuries

    Seven Eagles sat out Wednesday, including Barkley, Smith and Wicks

    Joe Milton may not get much help. The Eagles’ official Wednesday report lists seven players as did not participate: running back Saquon Barkley (hamstring and back), receivers DeVonta Smith, Dontayvion Wicks and Britain Covey, linebacker Jihaad Campbell, cornerback Jonathan Jones and defensive back Michael Carter II, per Eagles.com. Kicker Jake Elliott and edge rushers Jonathan Greenard and Jalyx Hunt were limited. McKee appeared with a chest injury but was full. Smith and Wicks have missed two straight games and appear on track to miss a third. Barkley, who sat out Wednesday and Thursday last week before playing on a pitch count, took 22 snaps against Washington after 10 against Dallas. Campbell missed the Commanders game, so Jeremiah Trotter Jr. could start again. Sources differ on Jones: Eagles.com says hamstring, PhillyVoice says knee. One betting page listed Smith and Wicks as questionable, which is not an official designation, so it was left out.

    Read at Eagles.com →
  3. Opponent · Giants

    Giants lead the East, but Skattebo and Carter sat out Wednesday

    The Giants (4-2-1) lead the NFC East coming off their bye, and their first report of the week listed two players who did not practice: running back Cam Skattebo (hamstring) and edge rusher Abdul Carter (illness), per Giants.com. Five were limited, including left tackle Andrew Thomas (groin) and right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor (calf). Tight end Theo Johnson, tackle Marcus Mbow and lineman DJ Reader were full. John Harbaugh said Carter was home resting and “should be fine,” per SI. Skattebo is “day-to-day,” and if he cannot go, Devin Singletary is in line to start, per the Trentonian. Cornerback Greg Newsome II, who missed the Houston game with a concussion, is expected back. Harbaugh said Wednesday that “word travels fast” on Milton and expects the same Eagles offense, and J.J. McCarthy called Milton “uber talented.” Eagles defensive coordinator Vic Fangio called the McCarthy trade “a steal,” though one transcript has him saying sixth-round when the pick was a fifth. The Giants have lost 13 straight in Philadelphia, but Harbaugh is 15-3 after a bye.

  4. The line · Odds

    The Giants are 3 to 3.5-point favorites, even after the QB switch

    The Giants are road favorites, and the Milton news has not moved the number much. A Thursday-morning Scores & Odds table shows New York at minus-3.5 at DraftKings (minus-102), bet365, Fanatics and Kalshi, and minus-3 at BetMGM, Caesars and FanDuel. Moneylines run from Giants minus-162 to minus-179. DraftKings has the total at 39.5 and Eagles plus-142, per Sports Illustrated, but the Scores & Odds header shows 38.5, a conflict I could not settle. SI and NBC Sports pulled their numbers Tuesday, before the quarterback change. NBC Sports says the look-ahead line with Hurts was Eagles minus-5.5 and 44.5, an eight-point swing, though its own text says minus-3 in one place and minus-3.5 in another. The Giants are 5-2 against the spread and the Eagles 1-7. It is the first time since 2009 the Giants are favored in Philadelphia. Lines may shift on Friday’s reports.

  5. Last game · Trade deadline

    Six straight losses, and the Nov. 10 deadline is two days after kickoff

    The Eagles (2-6) have lost six in a row, the last two by 17 points each, and they play the Giants before a Week 10 bye. Sunday’s 27-10 loss at Washington turned a 10-0 lead into 27 unanswered points, and special teams coordinator Michael Clay was asked Tuesday about Jake Elliott’s missed field goal and an illegal formation penalty on that unit, per Eagles.com. The trade deadline is Tuesday, Nov. 10, at 4 p.m. ET. Saquon Barkley says the team told him he will not be traded, per ESPN, and 97.5 The Fanatic calls Zack Baun unlikely to move. That outlet marks defensive tackle Moro Ojomo as a “Go” and tight end Dallas Goedert as a “Stay.” One reporter suggested McKee could now be on the trade block, which is his guess, not a report. The Eagles already traded center Cam Jurgens to Baltimore.
