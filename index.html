Game Recap · Week 2

Hurts to Cooper with 9 Seconds Left: Eagles Escape Nashville 24-20

The Eagles arrive in Chicago 2-0 thanks to one of the wildest finishes of the young season. Jalen Hurts threw two interceptions, including an early end-zone pick that Amani Hooker returned 92 yards and set up a Titans touchdown, and another on an underthrown deep ball to DeVonta Smith that led to a field goal and a 20-17 Tennessee lead with 1:56 left. With no timeouts, Hurts then drove Philadelphia 65 yards in nine plays. Smith kept the drive alive with a third-and-6 catch along the sideline, and Hurts hit undrafted receiver Darius Cooper for a 3-yard touchdown, the first of his NFL career, with nine seconds remaining. Hurts finished 26-of-37 for 264 yards and two touchdowns, while Smith had 10 catches for 117 yards. The turf reportedly hit 157 degrees, and coach Nick Sirianni needed an IV afterward.

Injury · Eagles

Saquon Barkley's Stinger: "We'll Know More Later in the Week"

Running back Saquon Barkley's status is the biggest question on the Eagles' side. He suffered a stinger, a nerve injury in his left arm, on the opening play against Tennessee after being tackled by defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons. He headed to the medical tent, then the locker room, briefly returned in the second half, and was pulled again. Barkley finished with just four carries for nine yards and one catch for 11 yards. He told reporters he would be fine but would learn more later in the week, and he is set to get an MRI. In his absence, Tank Bigsby handled the lead role with 13 carries for 33 yards and a touchdown, and Will Shipley added seven carries for 24 yards. Whether Barkley suits up Monday night will likely hinge on the MRI results and how he practices.

Injury · Eagles

Dallas Goedert Exits with a Knee Injury, and Tight End Depth Gets Thin

Tight end Dallas Goedert left the win over the Titans in the second quarter with a knee injury and never returned. He went to the medical tent, was listed as questionable to return, and the team has not shared more details on the severity. That is a notable loss after his big opener, when he caught four passes for 77 yards and two touchdowns against Washington. With Goedert out, the Eagles leaned on backups Johnny Mundt and E.J. Jenkins, and depth at the position is a real concern because second-round pick Eli Stowers is on injured reserve. The injury also compounds the Barkley situation, meaning two of the Eagles' most important offensive weapons are uncertain heading into a short week on the road. Opponent Watch · Bears

Bears QB Caleb Williams Hurts His Hamstring: Is Tyson Bagent Next?

Chicago's Week 2 loss to Minnesota got worse late in the fourth quarter when quarterback Caleb Williams went down on a first-and-goal scramble with a right hamstring injury and was carted off. Head coach Ben Johnson said he was relieved it was not a knee, but warned a hamstring "could be pretty damaging as well," adding the team would know more after Monday's evaluation. Williams walked through the locker room on his own and was in good spirits. He was 15-of-26 for 138 yards and an interception before leaving. Backup Tyson Bagent, who started four games in 2023, stepped in and drove Chicago inside the red zone twice without scoring. If Williams cannot go, Bagent is the likely starter against a Philadelphia defense that got sacks from Jalen Carter, playing with a cast on his wrist, and Nolan Smith Jr. in Nashville. His practice participation this week will be the key signal.
